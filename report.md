## 3. Effect of coupling asymmetry on the spontaneous decay and entanglement of two atoms in a common cavity

- arXiv: `2611.26373v1`
- Published: 2026-11-19 16:35 UTC
- Authors: Yamen Hamdouni
- Categories: quant-ph
- Links: [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26373v1) | [pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26373v1)
- Score: 4
- Keyword hits: title=['entanglement'] abstract=['entanglement']

We investigate the dynamics and the spontaneous decay of two identical two-level atoms into the vacuum in a cavity at low temperature. We analytically derive the survival probability and the amplitudes of the state of the system by calculating the exact self-energy when the atoms are both initially excited. It is found that moderate asymmetry enhances the decay of the survival probability and increases the entanglement production in either lossless and lossy cavities.
